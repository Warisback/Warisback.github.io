Vokal

Assistive hardware · personal project

A voice device for people with speech disorders, built to give them independence in everyday conversation: a hand-held puck that captures speech and runs it through open-source voice models, with no phone and no internet needed. The ESP32-S3 firmware does dual-core 16 kHz capture, an AMOLED touch UI and an on-device HTTP API over its own WiFi access point; the voice pipeline is tuned for latency, and on the puck's own recordings a phrase round-trips through speech-to-text and back to speech in under a second of model time on a laptop CPU.

C · ESP32-S3 · ES8311 audio · faster-whisper · Piper TTS
