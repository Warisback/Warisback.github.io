Vokal

Assistive speech · on-device ML · in progress

A voice for people whose words are intact but whose voice cannot carry them, after a laryngectomy or as MND weakens it. Hold to talk: Whisper transcribes in the browser on the device itself, a guard strips the words the model invents on silence, and nothing is said aloud until the person checks the text and taps Speak. It began as an ESP32-S3 hand-held device feeding a laptop; next I am measuring and fine-tuning Whisper on dysarthric speech (TORGO).

JavaScript · Whisper · transformers.js · WebGPU · Python · faster-whisper

RV32I single-cycle CPU

Digital design · verification

A single-cycle RISC-V processor in SystemVerilog, checked instruction by instruction against a Python instruction-set simulator. All 40 applicable official riscv-tests and 220 constrained-random programs produce traces identical to the simulator, and 10 of 10 deliberately planted bugs are caught, one of them only by the random programs. One instruction retires every clock: 90,494 instructions in 90,494 cycles.

SystemVerilog · Icarus Verilog · GTKWave · Python ISS · riscv-tests

FignalAI

RTL verification · Cerebras × Google DeepMind hackathon

A self-healing RTL debug agent. Feed it a buggy Verilog module and its testbench: it runs the simulation, reads the waveform with a vision agent, debates the root cause across five parallel agents, patches the source and re-runs the simulator to prove the fix. The oracle is always the simulator, never an LLM.

Python · Verilog · Gemma on Cerebras · VCD parsing
